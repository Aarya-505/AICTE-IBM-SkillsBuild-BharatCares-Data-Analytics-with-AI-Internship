# Indian Road Congress (IRC) Engineering Standards & Decision Matrix

## Overview
In transportation engineering and urban planning, empirical crash statistics identify **high-risk corridors, hazardous junctions, and vulnerable road users**. However, physical civil interventions and pavement design must strictly adhere to the standards formulated by the **Indian Roads Congress (IRC)** and the **Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH)**.

---

## 1. Applicable Indian Road Congress (IRC) Codes

| IRC Standard | Title / Domain | Planning & Engineering Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IRC:37-2018** | *Guidelines for the Design of Flexible Pavements* | Design of bituminous layers (DBM, BC), sub-base, and subgrade stabilization based on Million Standard Axles (MSA) and California Bearing Ratio (CBR). |
| **IRC:58-2015** | *Guidelines for the Design of Plain Jointed Rigid Pavements for Highways* | Concrete pavement design for heavy axle loads, toll plaza deceleration zones, and urban intersections prone to asphalt rutting. |
| **IRC:SP:84-2019** | *Manual of Specifications and Standards for Four Laning of Highways* | Geometric design, median barriers, service roads, grade separators, and pedestrian underpasses on 4-lane highways. |
| **IRC:SP:87-2019** | *Manual of Specifications and Standards for Six Laning of Highways* | Express highway safety design, access-controlled interchanges, and incident management systems. |
| **IRC:67-2012** | *Code of Practice for Road Signs* | Standardized retroreflective signage (Mandatory, Cautionary, Informatory) for speed limits, curves, and pedestrian zones. |
| **IRC:35-2015** | *Code of Practice for Road Markings* | Thermoplastic lane lines, chevron markings, zebra crossings, and rumble strips. |
| **IRC:SP:88-2019** | *Manual on Road Safety Audit (RSA)* | Systematic engineering evaluation of road projects at Feasibility, Detailed Design, Construction, and Pre-Opening stages. |
| **IRC:SP:79-2019** | *Stone Matrix Asphalt (SMA)* | Rut-resistant gap-graded surfacing for heavy commercial traffic corridors. |
| **IRC:SP:81-2018** | *Guidelines for Micro-surfacing & Slurry Seal* | Preventive surface maintenance to restore skid resistance without structural overlay. |

## 2. Pavement Surface Treatment Selection Matrix

| Observed Hazard Pattern | Probable Road Distress / Mechanism | Recommended Material / Surface Intervention | Relevant IRC Code |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wet Weather Skidding on Curves / Approaches** | Low micro/macro-texture; polished aggregate; inadequate Skid Resistance Index. | **High-Friction Surface Treatment (HFST)** with calcined bauxite aggregate bonded with epoxy/polyurethane resin. | IRC:SP:88 / MoRTH Sec 500 |
| **Rutting & Shoving at Signalized Intersections** | Viscous creep under prolonged standing heavy axle loads in hot weather. | **Stone Matrix Asphalt (SMA)** with polymer-modified bitumen (PMB) or short **Rigid Concrete Inlay**. | IRC:SP:79 / IRC:58 |
| **Hydroplaning & Water Spray in Heavy Monsoons** | Inadequate cross-slope (camber) drainage or smooth impermeable surface. | **Porous Friction Course (PFC) / Open-Graded Friction Course (OGFC)**. | MoRTH Cl 506 / IRC:37 |
| **Loss of Aggregate Texture on Aged Arterials** | Bitumen binder oxidation and surface micro-cracking. | **Polymer-Modified Micro-surfacing (Type II/III)**. | IRC:SP:81 |

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Design Civil Engineering Checklist
1. **Soil Subgrade Testing:**
   - California Bearing Ratio (CBR) (4-day soaked).
   - Dynamic Cone Penetrometer (DCP) profile.
2. **Traffic & Axle Load Spectrum:**
   - 7-Day Classified Automatic Vehicle Counter & Classifier (AVCC).
   - Commercial Vehicles per Day (CVPD) (> 3 tonnes unladen weight).
   - Vehicle Damage Factor (VDF) via Weigh-in-Motion (WIM).
3. **Pavement Structural Condition:**
   - Falling Weight Deflectometer (FWD) deflection testing.
   - Network Survey Vehicle (NSV) for International Roughness Index (IRI).
4. **Safety & Sightline Audit:**
   - British Pendulum Number (BPN) wet skid resistance testing (target BPN > 55 on high-speed curves).
   - Stopping Sight Distance (SSD) and Intersection Sight Distance (ISD) verification.
